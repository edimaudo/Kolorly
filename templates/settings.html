About Kolorly

Kolorly is a color matching game that challenges your ability to identify and reproduce colors.

Each round displays a target color. Use the color picker to create the closest possible match before the timer expires.

Your performance is measured using color accuracy. The closer your selected color is to the target, the higher your score.
